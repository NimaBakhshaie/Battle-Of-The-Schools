The web can feel like a maze. Menus inside menus. Unfamiliar words. And too many places to click.

Meet Orbit: a calm guide that helps you learn any ordinary website by doing.

Just ask what you want to accomplish. Type your question, tap to talk, or simply say, “Hello Orbit.”

Orbit studies the page and gives you one clear next step. A purple highlight shows exactly where to go, while you stay in control of every click, every word, and every decision.

Take a wrong turn? No problem. Orbit looks again and adapts to where you are. Pause when you need a break, resume when you’re ready, or stop at any time.

Behind the scenes, Orbit can inspect a public page in a separate practice browser, rehearse safe navigation, and save evidence for the guidance it gives you. Your login, passwords, payments, and final sensitive actions always stay with you.

From government services and online shopping, to travel, job applications, and everyday tasks, Orbit helps make the web feel possible.

Orbit. A little less lost. A lot more possible.
